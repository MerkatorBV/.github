## About Merkator

Merkator is a geospatial engineering company delivering GIS, asset-management and
location-intelligence solutions to utilities, infrastructure operators and
public-sector organisations in the Netherlands and Belgium. Merkator combines Esri
ArcGIS platform expertise with in-house software development and contributes
actively to the open-source geospatial ecosystem.

### Open source

**[NetTopologySuite.Proofs](https://github.com/MerkatorBV/NetTopologySuite.Proofs)**
— a Rocq/Coq proof corpus accompanying NetTopologySuite, together with the
differential tooling that compares real geometry engines (NTS, GEOS) against an
extracted oracle and pictures the cases under scrutiny.

The corpus covers the numerical foundations that geometry engines depend on:
robust orientation and incircle predicates over IEEE-754 `binary64`, snap-rounding
and noding, overlay and DE-9IM relate, and exact curve types from the SQL/MM and
Simple Features standards.
